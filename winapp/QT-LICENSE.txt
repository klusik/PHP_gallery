Qt uses separately installed shared libraries and plugins in the common
_internal directory. Shut down WinApp and replace the relevant files
there with ABI-compatible builds, keeping their relative paths and filenames.
Start WinApp with the default Qt / PySide6 backend. If Legacy Tk is selected,
choose Qt / PySide6 in Settings and restart. The launcher accepts
ABI-compatible replacement libraries. The public uploader
source, build tooling and installer script are available in this repository:
https://github.com/klusik/PHP_gallery/tree/develop/winapp
