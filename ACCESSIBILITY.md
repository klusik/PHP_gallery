# Accessibility

PHP Gallery aims to make its public galleries and administration usable by as
many people as practical. Accessibility is part of feature design and review;
this statement is not a claim of conformance to a particular WCAG level.

The application is server-rendered PHP with JavaScript enhancements. Some
workflows have a normal form or no-JavaScript fallback, while interactive
features may depend on JavaScript. Behavior can therefore differ by page and
feature. The project does not publish a complete browser and assistive
technology support matrix, and automated checks do not establish that every
screen-reader, keyboard, touch, or magnification workflow is accessible.

When reporting a barrier, please use the Accessibility issue form or open a
GitHub issue. Include the page or control, what you were trying to do, the
observed result, and the expected result. If relevant, identify the browser,
operating system, assistive technology, and input method, including versions
when known. A short reproduction sequence is especially helpful. Do not include
private gallery links, account credentials, or other sensitive information.

The project will review reported barriers and consider their impact when
planning fixes. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for development and
verification guidance.
